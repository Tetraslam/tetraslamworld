Some examples of people, and lately models, quickly accomplishing ambitious things in AI. After [patrickcollison.com/fast](https://patrickcollison.com/fast).

- **The Riemann hypothesis.** Levent Alpöge's account of his work with Claude begins on October 6 2026. The proof was complete on November 16, 41 days later. He released it on November 20 as 17 million lines of Lean. The Lean and Mathlib communities certified the statement and the proof the next day. The problem had been open since 1859. *Source: [Alpöge](https://alpo.ge/remark.pdf), [Lean community](https://leanprover-community.github.io/blog/posts/riemann-hypothesis/).*

- **The 100 metres.** On August 26 2026 the humanoid robot Tiangong Ultra ran 100 metres in 8.64 seconds. The winning time at the same event a year earlier was 21.50 seconds. The human record is 9.58. *Source: [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/sports/robot-olympics-ends-with-humanoids-864-second-100m-nearly-second-faster-than-2026-08-26/).*

- **Colossus.** In 2024 xAI built a 100,000-GPU training cluster in Memphis in 122 days. Training began 19 days after the first rack arrived. It doubled the cluster to 200,000 GPUs in a further 92 days. The first 210 megawatts of its second site took 91 days. *Source: [NVIDIA](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/spectrum-x-ethernet-networking-xai-colossus), [xAI](https://x.ai/colossus), [SemiAnalysis](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/elon-is-doing-it-again-spacex-will).*

- **Anthropic.** Incorporated in January 2021. Its annualized revenue was about $1 billion in January 2025, $47 billion in May 2026, $71 billion on September 30 2026 and $81 billion on October 31 2026. It had about 5,300 employees. On November 18 2026 it raised $81 billion in the largest IPO on record, at a valuation of $1.5 trillion. *Source: [Anthropic's S-1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026074338/anthropicpbc-sx1ax3.htm), [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/anthropic-clinches-biggest-ever-ipo-with-81-billion-offering-2026-11-18/).*

- **The Jacobian conjecture.** Posed in 1939. On July 19 2026 Levent Alpöge posted a 216-character counterexample in three dimensions, found with Claude Fable 5 during the World Cup final. It had been verified by the next morning. The two-dimensional case remains open. *Source: [Alpöge](https://x.com/__alpoge__/status/2079028340955197566), [Terence Tao](https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2026/07/21/a-digestion-of-the-jacobian-conjecture-counterexample/).*

- **Bun.** Bun was 535,496 lines of Zig. In 2026 Jarred Sumner rewrote it in Rust in 11 days using Claude Code, at a cost of about $165,000. He estimates that three engineers would have needed a year. *Source: [Bun](https://bun.com/blog/bun-in-rust).*

- **A replacement for C103.** C103 is the niobium alloy used in rocket nozzles since Apollo. In 2026 Radical AI's autonomous lab made more than 300 new alloys in 16 weeks. In a torch test at Purdue, one of them survived 125 times better than C103. *Source: [Radical AI](https://www.radical-ai.com/news/purdue-applied-research-institute-confirms-superior-performance-of-radical-ai-materials).*

- **Navier–Stokes.** OpenAI launched about 10,000 agents on September 1 2026. 88 hours later they had a proof of finite-time blowup for the three-dimensional Navier–Stokes equations with a smooth external force. Formalizing it in Lean took another 17 hours. *Source: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/navier-stokes-solution/).*

- **Sume.** Reached $1 million in annualized revenue six days after launching in October 2026, with two people. *Source: [David Im](https://x.com/davidim/status/2115478546630295600).*

- **RSA-260.** A 260-digit challenge number published in 1991 and never factored. In September 2026 an engineer at Cognition, working with Devin, went from the first prompt to the factors in about three weeks, using about 4,900 GPU-days. *Source: [Cognition](https://cognition.com/blog/factoring-rsa-260).*

- **Skild.** In November 2026 a Skild robot learned to pot a plant from a single video of a person doing it. Recording started at 9:16 PM. The robot began the task autonomously at 9:27 PM. The company reached $100 million in annual recurring revenue ten months after its first commercial deployments. *Source: [Skild](https://www.skild.ai/blogs/s1), [Skild](https://www.skild.ai/blogs/skild-crosses-100m-arr).*

- **Cursor.** Founded in 2022. On June 16 2026 SpaceX agreed to buy it for $60 billion, the largest acquisition of a venture-backed startup on record. The deal closed on August 14. *Source: [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/spacex-buy-anysphere-60-billion-2026-06-16/), [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/sandycarter/2026/06/16/spacex-buys-cursor-in-largest-startup-acquisition-ever-at-60-billion/).*

- **Fermat's Last Theorem.** Starting on August 7 2026, Claude agents wrote the first complete computer-checked proof of Fermat's Last Theorem in 11 days. It is 13 million lines of Lean. In November, 1,000 agents repeated the formalization in 30 hours. *Source: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/formalizing-fermats-last-theorem), [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-2).*

- **Zircon.** In November 2026 Gemini agents rewrote the remaining three quarters of Fuchsia's Zircon kernel from C++ into about 800,000 lines of safe Rust in two weeks. It passes the kernel's unit tests and boots on hardware. *Source: [Google Research](https://research.google/blog/advancing-science-engineering-with-antigravitys-teamwork-feature/).*

- **Scorpion toxin binders.** In 64 hours Claude generated 318,484 protein designs against scorpion neurotoxins and selected 180 for testing. Outside laboratories confirmed 17 binders to four toxins. *Source: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/claude-scorpion-toxin-binder-design).*

- **The half marathon.** On April 19 2026 an autonomous humanoid robot won the Beijing robot half marathon in 50 minutes 26 seconds. The previous year's winner took 2 hours 40 minutes 42 seconds. *Source: [AP](https://www.npr.org/2026/04/20/g-s1-118086/humanoid-robot-half-marathon).*

- **The classification of finite simple groups.** The original proof runs to roughly 15,000 pages across more than 500 papers by about 100 mathematicians. In October 2026 Axiom's agents formalized it in 24 days, producing 43.1 million lines of Lean. *Source: [Axiom](https://axiommath.ai/research/the-enormous-theorem-formalized/).*

- **Claude Code.** Became generally available in May 2025. It reached $1 billion in annualized revenue in November 2025, six months later. *Source: [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropic-acquires-bun-as-claude-code-reaches-usd1b-milestone).*

- **Two rocket engines.** In December 2025 LEAP 71 hot-fired two 20-kilonewton methane engines, a bell nozzle and an aerospike, under three weeks after writing their specifications. Both were designed by its software without human intervention. *Source: [LEAP 71](https://leap71.com/2025/12/11/leap-71-hot-fires-two-orbital-class-methalox-engines-designed-autonomously-by-noyron/).*

- **ChatGPT.** Launched on November 30 2022. It had one million users five days later and an estimated 100 million two months later. OpenAI's annualized revenue reached $10 billion in June 2025, about 30 months after launch. *Source: [Sam Altman](https://x.com/sama/status/1599668808285028353), [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/technology/chatgpt-sets-record-fastest-growing-user-base-analyst-note-2023-02-01/), [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/openais-annualized-revenue-hits-10-billion-up-55-billion-december-2024-2025-06-09/).*

- **AlphaFold.** The AlphaFold database opened in July 2021 with about one million predicted protein structures. One year later it held more than 200 million, nearly every protein known to science. *Source: [DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/alphafold-reveals-the-structure-of-the-protein-universe/).*

- **Base44.** Maor Shlomo founded Base44 alone and sold it to Wix for $80 million six months later, in June 2025. *Source: [Wix](https://www.wix.com/press-room/home/post/wix-further-expands-into-vibe-coding-with-acquisition-of-base44-a-hyper-growth-startup-that-simplif).*

- **Qwen.** Alibaba launched the Qwen app on November 17 2025. It had 100 million monthly users within two months. *Source: [Alibaba](https://www.alibabagroup.com/document-1948497434959151104).*

- **Google's tokens.** Google processed 9.7 trillion tokens a month in May 2024, 480 trillion in May 2025 and more than 3.2 quadrillion in May 2026. *Source: [Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/sundar-pichai-io-2026/).*

- **OpenClaw.** Peter Steinberger wrote it as a weekend project in November 2025. Two months later it had more than 100,000 GitHub stars. *Source: [Steinberger](https://openclaw.ai/blog/introducing-openclaw).*

- **Stargate Abilene.** Construction started in June 2024. The first NVIDIA racks arrived in June 2025 and OpenAI was running workloads in July. By September 30 2025, two buildings and more than 200 megawatts were operational. *Source: [Crusoe](https://www.crusoe.ai/resources/newsroom/crusoe-announces-flagship-abilene-data-center-is-live).*

- **Genspark.** Reached $10 million in annualized revenue nine days after launching its agent in April 2025, with about 20 people. *Source: [Genspark](https://www.genspark.ai/blog/genspark-less-control-more-tools).*

- **The exponential time hypothesis.** OpenAI's system produced a disproof on October 16 2026. It was formalized in Lean on October 17. *Source: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/building-scientific-understanding-with-academic-communities/).*

- **Signal.** In November 2026 Google DeepMind's agents formally verified the core of Signal's cryptographic code, down to the compiled binary, in four weeks. The proof is more than six million lines of Lean. *Source: [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/blog/introducing-alphaproof-code/).*

- **Lovable.** Went from $1 million to $100 million in annual recurring revenue in eight months, with 45 employees. *Source: [Lovable](https://lovable.dev/blog/agent).*

- **Local smoothing.** OpenAI's system produced a proof of the local smoothing conjecture in every dimension on October 18 2026, which implies the Kakeya and restriction conjectures. It was formalized in Lean the following day. *Source: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/building-scientific-understanding-with-academic-communities/).*

- **Bloom Energy.** In 2025 Bloom delivered a fully operational on-site power system to Oracle in 55 days. *Source: [Bloom Energy](https://www.bloomenergy.com/news/bloom-energy-and-oracle-expand-strategic-partnership-to-deploy-up-to-2-8-gw-to-accelerate-ai-infrastructure-build-out/).*

- **World Labs.** Fei-Fei Li founded World Labs in 2024. On September 28 2026 AMD agreed to buy it for $8.2 billion. *Source: [AMD](https://ir.amd.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1299/amd-to-acquire-world-labs-to-advance-the-future-of-ai-compute), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/28/amd-will-acquire-fei-fei-lis-world-labs-for-8-2-billion/).*

- **Pocket.** An AI hardware device. Its annualized revenue went from $1 million to $100 million in seven months, with 270,000 devices in use by August 2026. *Source: [Pocket](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/heypocketai_pocket-just-crossed-100m-in-annualized-run-activity-7495890232765100032-dFDS).*

- **The cycle double cover conjecture.** Open for about 50 years. In July 2026 GPT-5.6, running 64 subagents, produced a proof in just under an hour. It has since been checked in Lean. *Source: [OpenAI's prompt](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/04d1d1e4-bc75-476a-97cf-49055cd98d31/cdc_prompt.pdf), [Lean proof](https://github.com/openai/cdc-lean).*

- **Agents.** In October 2025 agents used 8% of the tokens on OpenRouter. In October 2026 they used 76%. By its estimate, February 6 2026 was the last day people used more tokens than agents. *Source: [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/data/october-2026), [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/data/newsletter/the-token-state-of-play).*

- **The Dinitz–Garg–Goemans conjecture.** Open for 30 years. In July 2026 Dmitry Rybin found a counterexample with GPT-5.6 Pro in 5.5 hours, using four prompts totalling fewer than 60 words. *Source: [Rybin](https://x.com/DmitryRybin1/status/2079906232069177643), [New Scientist](https://www.newscientist.com/article/2580932-extremely-basic-ai-prompt-cracks-decades-old-maths-problem/).*

- **Manus.** Reached $100 million in annual recurring revenue in December 2025, eight months after launch, with 105 employees. *Source: [Manus](https://manus.im/blog/manus-100m-arr).*

- **The Linux kernel.** In October 2026 an OpenAI pipeline produced 2,308 candidate vulnerabilities in the Linux kernel in 12 hours. The full run yielded 948 working root exploits, each with a patch, for $1.2 million. *Source: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/a-thousand-fewer-paths-to-root).*

- **Jalapeño.** OpenAI and Broadcom took an inference chip from initial design to tape-out in nine months. *Source: [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/openai-broadcom-jalapeno-inference-chip/).*

- **The Aaronson–Ambainis conjecture.** Open since 2008. In November 2026 a research system at Physical Superintelligence had a complete proof, accepted by Lean, on the third day of its run. *Source: [Physical Superintelligence](https://psi.inc/research/aaronson-ambainis/).*

- **Emergent.** Reached $100 million in annualized revenue in February 2026, eight months after launch. *Source: [AWS](https://aws.amazon.com/aws-startups/learn/from-yc-to-aws-how-emergent-hit-100m-usd-annualized-revenue-in-8-months/).*

- **Firefox.** In February 2026 Claude found 22 vulnerabilities in Firefox in two weeks, 14 of them high severity. It found the first after 20 minutes. *Source: [Mozilla](https://blog.mozilla.org/en/firefox/hardening-firefox-anthropic-red-team/).*

- **721 manuscripts.** On October 6 2026 OpenAI published results on 373 open problems, drawn from about 4,000 posed to a single model. ## What's going on?

- METR, given inside access to Anthropic, [estimates](https://metr.org/blog/2026-11-05-anthropic-ai-rd-acceleration-assessment/) that since October 2025 Anthropic's index of its models' capabilities has grown at 1.9 times the earlier rate, so that two years of progress took one. It judges that most of the acceleration is due to AI assisting with research and development. Anthropic's safety policy treats this kind of acceleration as a key risk indicator.

- Anthropic's [IPO filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026074338/anthropicpbc-sx1ax3.htm) says Claude wrote more than 90% of the contributions to its codebase in September 2026.

- OpenAI [reports](https://openai.com/index/research-acceleration-jagged-frontier/) that between May and October 2026 its researchers went from running 0.5 agent-workdays per person per day to 5.7, and that the share of two-to-four-hour research tasks its agents complete with no human intervention rose from 23% in March to 46% in October.

- METR's [measurements](https://metr.org/time-horizons/) of the length of task a model can complete half the time were doubling about every three months as of January 2026. In May 2026 they reached roughly 16 hours, the most its task suite can measure.

- Epoch AI [finds](https://epoch.ai/publications/the-plunging-price-of-thought) that the cost of a given level of AI performance has fallen about 47% per quarter since 2023, halving roughly every three months. That is 13 times cheaper per year, four times the rate at which DNA sequencing fell.

- In July 2026, 1,386 employees of frontier AI companies signed [Pacing the Frontier](https://www.pacingthefrontier.com/). The signatories include Dario Amodei, Ilya Sutskever, Shane Legg, Jakub Pachocki, Jared Kaplan, Mark Chen, John Schulman, Shengjia Zhao, Jack Clark and Chris Olah: the chief scientists of OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, Meta AI and Thinking Machines among them. The statement says the companies "believe they could be close to automating AI research" and asks the U.S. government to support an international effort to build the tools "needed to deliberately pace the frontier of automated AI development".
